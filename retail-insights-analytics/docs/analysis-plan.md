# Analysis Plan

## 1. Executive KPIs

Calculate and document:
- Total sales
- Total gross profit
- Total quantity sold
- Gross profit margin = gross profit / sales (only when definitions and denominators are valid)

For each KPI, include the reporting period, currency, comparison baseline, and any exclusions.

## 2. Product performance

Compare sales, gross profit, and quantity by category and sub-category. Identify high-contribution and low-contribution groups, then investigate whether high sales also translate into healthy gross profit.

## 3. Geographic performance

Compare sales and quantity by state. Validate geographic mapping and normalize comparisons where appropriate (for example, per order or per customer if those fields are available).

## 4. Time-series performance

Review monthly, quarterly, and yearly sales and gross-profit trends. Look for seasonality, sustained changes, and unusual peaks or drops. Avoid causal claims unless supported by additional evidence.

## 5. Business recommendations

For every recommendation, state:
1. The observed evidence and exact metric.
2. The business implication.
3. A practical action.
4. A limitation or follow-up analysis.
